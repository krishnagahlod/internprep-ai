# TasteSkill & Anti-Slop Design Guidelines

## Core Principles (Linear / Vercel Tier-1 Standard)
1. **Never use AI cliches:**
   - NO em-dashes ("—") in marketing copy.
   - NO AI buzzwords: "Unlock", "Empower", "Revolutionize", "Seamless", "Supercharge", "It's not X, it's Y".
   - NO decorative emoji headers (⚡, ✨, 🚀, 💡, 🔥) on every card or badge.
   - NO `Sparkles` or `Zap` icons as primary brand decoration.

2. **Color & Background Discipline:**
   - Never use pure black `#000000`. Use deep obsidian (`#08090A`, `#0C0D0E`, `#121316`).
   - NO pastel rainbow gradient blur blobs, radial background spots, or multi-color text gradients.
   - Limit color palette to:
     - Neutral base: Dark obsidian / charcoal
     - Text: High-contrast white (`#EDEDED`) and muted neutral (`#A1A1AA`)
     - Single precision accent: Technical Emerald (`#10B981`) or Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`) or Crisp White.

3. **Geometry & Depth:**
   - NO bubbly `rounded-3xl` (24px-32px) cards. Use sharp, precise `rounded-lg` (8px) and `rounded-xl` (12px).
   - Use 1px crisp borders (`border-white/10` or `border-zinc-800/80`) and subtle top inner highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`).
   - Avoid heavy blurred multi-color drop shadows (`shadow-violet-500/25`). Use understated structural elevation.

4. **Layout Architecture:**
   - Avoid generic 3-card columns or cookie-cutter bento boxes.
   - Prefer structured 2-column deep-dive interactive feature sections with realistic product previews.
   - Always embed authentic interactive sandboxes rather than static illustration cards.

5. **Typography & Hierarchy:**
   - Use high-clarity Display typography for titles, crisp neutral Sans for body, and `JetBrains Mono` / Monospace for timestamps, metrics, scores, code, and rubrics.
   - Generous line height and clear hierarchical rhythm.
